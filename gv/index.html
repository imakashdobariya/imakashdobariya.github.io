What is Gnan Vidhi?

Gnan Vidhi is a scientific process to attain experiential knowledge of the Self. It establishes the permanent separation of the Self from the body, mind, and speech.

FAQs
  • To take Gnan, you do not need to change your religion or your guru.
  • Gnan is priceless; therefore, there is no cost to obtain it.

  • An experiment of the Science of separation for attaining Self-realization, different from question-and-answer satsang.
  • The same Knowledge of the Self (Atma Gnan) that manifested in Param Pujya Dada Bhagwan in 1958 can be attained today through His grace and the blessings of Pujya Niruma, through the medium of Atma Gnani Pujya Deepakbhai.

  • To become free from the cycle of birth and death.
  • To awaken one’s own Self.
  • To experience peace and happiness in one’s home, family life, and work.

  • The awareness of the Self arises.
  • One receives the keys to complete worldly life with right understanding.
  • Wrong beliefs go away.
  • New karma stops being bound and old karma continue to discharge.
  • The demerit karma of infinite lifetimes are destroyed.

  • The Gnan Vidhi is the result of the Gnani’s grace and blessings; therefore, it is essential to be present in person.
  • TV and video satsangs of Pujya Niruma and Pujya Deepakbhai, as well as Dada’s books, can prepare you for receiving Gnan, but they cannot grant Self-realization.
  • Any means of acquiring knowledge may bring peace, but to awaken one’s own Self, one must come in person and take Gnan. For example, a lamp drawn in a book cannot give light, but only a lamp that is actually lit can give light.
